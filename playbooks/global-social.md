# Global Social Playbook｜海外社媒

Build monthly content around brand events, product KSP, verified data, customer/owner proof, industry moments, cultural moments, short video and platform-native formats.

Rules:
- adapt to local market and platform
- use local-market product facts
- obtain rights/consent for testimonials and identifiable user content
- do not imply participation, sponsorship or partnership when merely newsjacking an event
- verify music/media rights and regional availability
- separate draft data templates from final approved numbers
