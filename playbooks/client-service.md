# Client Service Playbook｜客户沟通

1. Capture the client's exact request.
2. Identify the underlying need: speed, certainty, quality, resource, accountability, approval or budget.
3. Separate confirmed facts from unknowns.
4. Check whether the reply creates a commitment on scope, price, timing, quality or responsibility.
5. Classify risk.
6. Draft the shortest complete response.
7. If RED, label `HUMAN_REQUIRED` and do not send automatically.

Useful structure:

`understand → conclusion → necessary reason → executable next step → boundary`

Avoid generic customer-service language, defensive essays and promises that have no owner or verified due date.
