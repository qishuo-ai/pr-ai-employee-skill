# Crisis Communications Playbook｜危机传播

## First response
Establish:
- what is confirmed
- what remains unknown
- affected stakeholders
- propagation/severity level
- operational actions underway
- interim holding line
- authorized approvers/spokesperson
- next update time

## Never
- speculate about causes
- invent numbers
- assign blame without evidence
- accept liability without authority
- allow multiple conflicting spokespersons
- automatically send a crisis statement to media/public channels

Crisis statements, responsibility attribution and media responses are RED by default.
