# Pitch & Proposal Playbook｜竞标提案

## Requirement matrix
Before strategy, map every mandatory brief requirement, scoring item, deliverable, page/time constraint, commercial condition and missing input.

## Strategic chain
`business problem → evidence → tension → brand opportunity → strategic choice → narrative → mechanism → actions → measurement`

## Delivery chain
`phase → action → owner → dependency → timing → KPI → risk → acceptance`

## Quality gates
- A creative theme is not automatically a strategy.
- A list of tactics is not a communication mechanism.
- Every key claim needs evidence or an explicit hypothesis label.
- Media/KOL selections must have assigned jobs.
- The proposal must be both presentable and executable.
- If a page limit exists, meet it exactly without destroying logic.
